the build and CI files and the other VibeQM records first, and fill every section
they answer, naming the source in a few words ("from CLAUDE.md"). Put everything
they don't answer in **Open questions** and ask the person a few at a time, with
options and your recommendation first. Never invent a date, a budget or a name.
