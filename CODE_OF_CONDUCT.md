# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticise ideas and not people,
and keep discussions on topic. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language
are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project
meets.

To report a problem, email <hello@vibeqm.com>. Reports
are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and
block people, who break these rules.
